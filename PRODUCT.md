# Product

## Register

product

## Users
Solo creators producing Vietnamese horror story audio and video content for YouTube, Shorts, and similar social platforms. They work in repeated production sessions and need fast, inspectable controls for story generation, TTS, scene images, subtitles, video render, cutting, outro append, and publishing preparation.

## Product Purpose
LucyLab Story Studio turns a story idea or pasted story into finished audio/video assets through a local-first pipeline. Success means creators can recover old sessions, see progress clearly, keep generated assets organized, and fix provider/account issues without leaving the tool.

## Brand Personality
Focused, technical, and cinematic. The interface should feel like a production console for repeat work, not a marketing page or toy generator.

## Anti-references
Avoid decorative landing-page layouts, oversized hero treatment inside the working app, vague loading states, hidden pipeline state, and settings that require fragile manual terminal work when the app can expose them safely.

## Design Principles
- Make long-running jobs observable with concrete state, progress, and actionable errors.
- Keep power controls close to the workflow but visually contained so the main pipeline stays scannable.
- Preserve local-first autonomy: read local config and expose repair tools instead of forcing repeated manual setup.
- Treat every story as a recoverable project/session with visible assets and metadata.
- Prefer familiar product controls over ornamental UI.

## Accessibility & Inclusion
Use high-contrast text on the dark interface, keyboard-reachable controls, visible focus states, and reduced-motion-friendly transitions. Error messages should explain the next action in plain language.
